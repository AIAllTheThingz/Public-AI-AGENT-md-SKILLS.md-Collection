# Design Consistency Standard

## Purpose

Use coherent language and patterns while allowing evidence-backed contextual differences.

## Required behavior

- Reuse reviewed design-system components, tokens, terminology, interaction patterns, and platform conventions where applicable.
- Record justified deviations and their user, accessibility, compatibility, maintenance, and migration impacts.
- Keep equivalent actions, states, feedback, and errors semantically and behaviorally consistent.
- Do not prioritize visual uniformity over user need, accessibility, security, or native platform behavior.

## Required evidence

Pattern inventory or design-system references, deviation decisions, cross-journey review, and accessibility evidence.

## Completion gate

Do not claim consistency from visual resemblance alone; interaction semantics, state, terminology, and accessibility must also agree where applicable.
