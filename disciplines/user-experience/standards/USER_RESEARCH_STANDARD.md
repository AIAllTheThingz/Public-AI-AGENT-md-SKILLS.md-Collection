# User Research Standard

## Purpose

Produce ethical, attributable user evidence and make absence of research visible.

## Required behavior

- Define the research question, method, target population, recruitment boundaries, consent, data handling, analysis, and decision use.
- Record sampling and accessibility limitations, excluded populations, researcher influence, and conflicting findings.
- Protect participant identity and sensitive data according to privacy and security requirements.
- Never invent participants, observations, quotations, behaviors, counts, or results.
- Label synthetic personas, simulated interviews, inferred needs, heuristic findings, and agent-generated material as assumptions or design inputs.
- Record the research state as `Performed` only when actual research was completed and attributable evidence exists; otherwise use `NotRun` when actual research did not occur, `Blocked` when a prerequisite prevents it, or justified `NotApplicable` only when research is genuinely unnecessary.

## Required evidence

Approved plan, consent and handling record, method, de-identified findings, limitations, date, owner, and decision links—or an explicit accurate non-run state.

## Completion gate

Do not claim user validation from assumptions, stakeholder opinion, analytics alone, synthetic data, or an agent-generated narrative.
