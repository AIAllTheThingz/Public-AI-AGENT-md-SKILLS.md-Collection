# Usability Standard

## Purpose

Evaluate whether specified users can achieve specified goals effectively and efficiently in a stated context, including relevant satisfaction evidence.

## Required behavior

- Define users, goals, tasks, context, method, measures, thresholds where authorized, and evidence limitations.
- Select measures proportionately, such as completion, error, assistance, comprehension, time, confidence, or satisfaction; do not require every measure.
- Include representative critical, infrequent, error, and recovery tasks where risk warrants.
- Keep accessibility conformance and assistive-technology evidence as a distinct applicable workstream.

## Required evidence

Usability plan and results with context, participants or method, task outcomes, limitations, findings, severity, and follow-up decisions.

## Completion gate

Do not generalize beyond the tested users, tasks, context, interface version, or evidence quality.
