# Interaction Design Standard

## Purpose

Define predictable actions, state, feedback, constraints, and recovery.

## Required behavior

- Specify triggers, preconditions, system state, feedback, completion, cancellation, reversal, and recovery for material interactions.
- Prevent accidental destructive or irreversible actions and make consequences understandable before commitment.
- Define applicable loading, empty, validation, error, timeout, permission, offline, partial-success, and conflict states.
- Apply security, privacy, accessibility, and platform controls without using visual convenience to weaken them.

## Required evidence

Interaction specification or prototype, state model, edge cases, requirement links, accessibility review, and validation results.

## Completion gate

Do not report an interaction complete while material states, consequences, feedback, or recovery are implicit.
