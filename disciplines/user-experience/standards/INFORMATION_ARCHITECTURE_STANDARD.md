# Information Architecture Standard

## Purpose

Make information findable, understandable, and appropriately disclosed in context.

## Required behavior

- Define content groups, hierarchy, labels, navigation, search, filtering, orientation, and progressive disclosure as applicable.
- Use user language supported by evidence; label inferred terminology as an assumption.
- Preserve consistent mental models across journeys without hiding security, privacy, legal, or operational consequences.
- Validate representative findability and comprehension and apply Accessibility semantics separately.

## Required evidence

Sitemap or structure, taxonomy and label rationale, task mappings, validation state, limitations, and accessibility cross-reference.

## Completion gate

Do not report the structure complete until critical information and actions are locatable, understandable, and reviewed in applicable journeys.
