# User Experience Adoption Checklist

- [ ] Intended users, goals, contexts, journeys, requirements, owners, and risk are identified.
- [ ] Product Management, Accessibility, Architecture, Testing, Security, Privacy, and Documentation are composed as applicable.
- [ ] Research state is `Performed`, `NotRun`, `Blocked`, or justified `NotApplicable`; it and the UX-validation state are recorded independently, and no synthetic assumption is presented as observed evidence.
- [ ] UX-validation state is separate from per-method and overall outcome; a UX-validated claim has an overall `Pass` supported by every applicable method and claim, and failed, blocked, not-run, missing, stale, or different-version evidence remains fail-visible.
- [ ] Journeys cover tasks, touchpoints, interactions, results, failures, and recovery where applicable.
- [ ] Information architecture, interaction states, prototype boundaries, consistency, and usability criteria are defined.
- [ ] Participant consent, privacy, accessibility, and sensitive-data handling are governed.
- [ ] Evidence locations, validation methods, limitations, exclusions, review triggers, and unresolved risks are recorded.
