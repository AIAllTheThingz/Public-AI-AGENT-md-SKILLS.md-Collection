# Product Management Adoption Checklist

## Applicability and ownership

- [ ] Applicability, product scope, decision authority, requirement owner, reviewer, and evidence owner are recorded.
- [ ] Applicable governance, profile, UX, architecture, security, testing, accessibility, SRE, and delivery packages are selected.

## Definition

- [ ] Problem, intended users or actors, desired outcomes, facts, assumptions, constraints, boundaries, exclusions, and unknowns are explicit.
- [ ] Functional and applicable nonfunctional requirements have stable identifiers, sources, owners, priorities, and acceptance criteria.
- [ ] MVP inclusions, exclusions, dependencies, and learning goals are reviewed.

## Evidence

- [ ] Traceability covers applicable lifecycle stages and uses honest states.
- [ ] Acceptance results are recorded separately from evidence states; every accepted requirement has a current explicit `Pass` for each applicable criterion on the exact reviewed artifact and conditions.
- [ ] Research provenance is `Performed` only with attributable completed research evidence, or accurately `NotRun`, `Blocked`, or justified `NotApplicable`; no standalone Product Management record invents a state or implies that User Experience was selected.
- [ ] Any `production` lifecycle claim retains the complete candidate and approval boundary and has current primary evidence that the exact approved artifact and configuration were successfully deployed into and are operating within the stated production environment and scope; readiness or approval alone is not treated as deployment evidence.
- [ ] Evidence locations, review triggers, exceptions, and remaining risks are defined.
