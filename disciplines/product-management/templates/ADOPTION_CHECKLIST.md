# Product Management Adoption Checklist

## Applicability and ownership

- [ ] Applicability, product scope, decision authority, requirement owner, reviewer, and evidence owner are recorded.
- [ ] Applicable governance, profile, UX, architecture, security, testing, accessibility, SRE, and delivery packages are selected.

## Definition

- [ ] Problem, intended users or actors, desired outcomes, facts, assumptions, constraints, boundaries, exclusions, and unknowns are explicit.
- [ ] Functional and applicable nonfunctional requirements have stable identifiers, sources, owners, priorities, and acceptance criteria.
- [ ] MVP inclusions, exclusions, dependencies, and learning goals are reviewed.

## Evidence

- [ ] Traceability covers applicable lifecycle stages and uses honest states.
- [ ] Research and operational claims are supported or marked `NotRun`, `Blocked`, or `NotApplicable` with rationale.
- [ ] Evidence locations, review triggers, exceptions, and remaining risks are defined.
