# Problem Definition Standard

## Purpose

Define the problem before selecting or presenting a solution.

## Required behavior

- Describe the current condition, affected users or actors, context, impact, boundaries, and explicit non-problems.
- Record supporting evidence and its source, freshness, representativeness, and limitations.
- Label proposed explanations, synthetic examples, and untested beliefs as assumptions.
- Assign owners and follow-up decisions to material unknowns.

## Required evidence

Problem statement, source references, affected scope, facts-assumptions-unknowns ledger, and review record.

## Completion gate

Do not report problem definition complete when evidence, scope, affected actors, or material unknowns remain hidden.
