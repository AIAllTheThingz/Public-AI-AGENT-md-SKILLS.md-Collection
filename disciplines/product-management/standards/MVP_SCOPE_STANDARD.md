# MVP Scope Standard

## Purpose

Define the smallest coherent scope capable of producing the intended evidence without disguising omissions.

## Required behavior

- Identify included outcomes, requirements, users, workflows, supported conditions, and quality boundaries.
- List exclusions, deferred requirements, experimental behavior, and unsupported conditions explicitly.
- Preserve safety, security, privacy, accessibility, data integrity, and evidence controls required by risk.
- Define what evidence will decide whether to continue, change, or stop.

## Required evidence

Approved scope, inclusion-exclusion mapping, deferred-item owners, risk decisions, and learning criteria.

## Completion gate

Do not report MVP scope approved until inclusions, exclusions, minimum quality boundaries, and decision evidence are explicit.
