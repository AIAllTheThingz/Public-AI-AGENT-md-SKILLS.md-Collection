# Product Vision Standard

## Purpose

Define a reviewable direction without turning aspiration into unsupported fact.

## Required behavior

- Identify the intended users or actors, problem space, desired value, boundaries, and explicit exclusions.
- State outcome signals and time horizon without inventing targets or demand.
- Separate confirmed facts, sourced evidence, assumptions, constraints, and unknowns.
- Link material changes in vision to affected requirements and decisions.

## Required evidence

A dated, owned vision record with sources, assumptions, exclusions, outcome signals, review triggers, and unresolved questions.

## Completion gate

Do not report the vision defined until its audience, intended value, evidence basis, boundaries, assumptions, and owner are explicit.
