# Prioritization Standard

## Purpose

Make ordering decisions reviewable and proportionate to evidence and risk.

## Required behavior

- Define criteria before ranking work and include outcome value, risk, dependency, effort uncertainty, urgency, and learning value where applicable.
- Keep mandatory safety, legal, security, privacy, accessibility, and operational obligations out of discretionary trade-offs.
- Record evidence quality, assumptions, conflicts, decision owner, and rejected alternatives.
- Reprioritize when evidence or material constraints change.

## Required evidence

Prioritized requirement list, criteria, rationale, evidence limitations, dependencies, and decision owner.

## Completion gate

Do not present priority as objective fact when it depends on assumptions, missing evidence, or an unrecorded authority decision.
