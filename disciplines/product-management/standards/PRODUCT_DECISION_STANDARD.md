# Product Decision Standard

## Purpose

Keep material product decisions attributable, reversible where practical, and connected to evidence.

## Required behavior

- Record decision, context, alternatives, evidence, assumptions, constraints, trade-offs, owner, date, and affected requirements.
- Distinguish recommendation, review, approval, and accountable authority.
- Define review triggers and reversal, migration, or deprecation implications.
- Preserve superseded decisions and explain the replacement rather than rewriting history.

## Required evidence

Dated decision record with owner, reviewers, authority, linked requirements, alternatives, evidence limitations, and review trigger.

## Completion gate

Do not treat an undocumented preference, agent recommendation, or prototype choice as an approved product decision.
