# User Outcome Standard

## Purpose

Connect intended users or actors to goals, journeys, results, and measurable evidence.

## Required behavior

- Define each material actor, goal, current barrier, intended result, and negative or unintended outcome.
- Prefer outcome measures over feature delivery counts and document measurement limitations.
- Trace outcomes to requirements and acceptance criteria without fabricating user research.
- Reassess outcomes when users, context, evidence, or scope changes materially.

## Required evidence

Actor-outcome mapping, measurement plan, assumptions, research state, and linked requirements.

## Completion gate

Do not claim an outcome achieved until representative evidence exists; otherwise record the accurate state, including `NotRun` or `Blocked`.
