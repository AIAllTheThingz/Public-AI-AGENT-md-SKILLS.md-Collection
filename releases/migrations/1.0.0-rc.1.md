# Migration to 1.0.0-rc.1 from 0.10.0

## Who is affected

Adopters intentionally evaluating the `1.0.0-rc.1` compatibility candidate from published `v0.10.0`, existing SRE or Testing package adopters, existing canonical-profile adopters whose scope satisfies a Product Management or User Experience predicate, all existing Agent Working Method adopters, completion-template or completion-result schema consumers, and maintainers reviewing the intended first stable repository contract.

Existing `v0.10.0` consumers are not forced to move to this prerelease.

## Required actions

1. Record the exact `1.0.0-rc.1` source commit or published prerelease tag used for evaluation; do not relabel `v0.10.0` evidence as RC evidence.
2. Existing SRE adopters must add `SRE-READINESS-006` and `SRE-SCALING-007` to tailored instructions; assess every production-readiness and scaling-strategy area; keep Data migration, Privacy, and Security as independent decisions when applicable; record per-area plus overall readiness evidence for the exact candidate and operating scope; record representative scaling validation that misses authorized criteria as `Failed` rather than unresolved; and withhold an overall scaling-strategy `Verified` claim until every applicable area is `Verified` with no `Applicable`, `NotRun`, `Blocked`, or `Failed` area.
3. Existing Testing adopters must add `TEST-PERFORMANCE-006` to tailored instructions and assess baseline, load, stress, spike, soak or endurance, scaling, failure-under-load, and recovery-under-load applicability. For each test type, record execution state separately from `Pass`, `Fail`, `Blocked`, or justified `NotApplicable` outcome; record the owner, explicit execution authorization and safeguards, safe stop conditions, representative workload and environment, authorized criteria, primary results, and recovery evidence. A performance or scalability validation claim requires every applicable test type to have state `Tested` and outcome `Pass`.
4. Existing profile adopters of `AI_AGENT_APPLICATION`, `CLI_TOOL`, `DATA_PIPELINE`, `DESKTOP_APPLICATION`, `INTERNAL_AUTOMATION`, `MOBILE_APPLICATION`, `MULTI_TENANT_SAAS`, `PUBLIC_LIBRARY`, `SECURITY_TOOL`, `SERVERLESS_FUNCTION`, `WEB_API`, `WEB_APPLICATION`, or `WORKER_SERVICE` must re-evaluate every selected primary and secondary profile against its conditional Product Management and User Experience predicates.
5. Select Product Management when the affected profile scope defines or materially changes its product capability, outcome, requirements, supported scope, MVP boundary, acceptance criteria, or product decisions. Select User Experience when the affected scope owns human goals, journeys, tasks, prompts, approvals, interactions, recovery states, interfaces, or UX-validation claims. If a predicate is not satisfied, retain a scope-specific omission rationale; profile selection alone is insufficient to select either overlay.
6. When a predicate is satisfied, add the corresponding `product-management` or `user-experience` discipline to the project manifest and composed standards, then synchronize the decision across the complete profile README, adoption checklist and example, root and nested `AGENTS.md`, tailoring record, and completion evidence. For a selected User Experience package, record validation state separately from per-method and overall outcome and withhold a UX-validated claim unless the overall outcome is `Pass` and every applicable method and claim succeeds for the exact interface version and representative conditions. `--include-profile-required` does not select conditional overlays automatically.
7. Treat `languages/csharp` as the only package in the initial stable package promise. All other packages remain `baseline` unless a later evidence-backed promotion says otherwise.
8. If automation consumes schema `$id` values, stable template paths, tool entry paths, exit codes, JSON result fields, or release artifact names, retain those contracts or document any explicit candidate incompatibility before adoption.
9. Re-run repository and project validation appropriate to every adopted package after changing the pinned standards source. Adopters unable to complete newly required package and evidence work must retain their prior pin or record the migration as `Blocked`; they must not claim conformance to this RC.
10. Existing Agent Working Method adopters must add GOV-WORK-011 through GOV-WORK-014 and retain GOV-WORK-003 scope discipline. Track the same objective or underlying blocker across the whole active task or change and all tools, tasks, sessions, agents, and strategies; count one budget-consuming attempt as one execution action directed at the objective or blocker, ending after observable effects are reconciled with a Failed or Indeterminate result, whether mutating or read-only, and count each further execution action as a retry even within the same command sequence, plan, workflow, tool, agent, or strategy. Every retry records a causally relevant material change and why that change creates a concrete reason it may succeed; generic justification, rewording, rerunning, changing tools or agents, or delegating the same approach is insufficient. Successful read-only discovery, actual-state reconciliation, and non-state-changing validation that only gather evidence do not consume budget, while Failed or Indeterminate read-only execution directed at the objective or blocker consumes budget, and Successful objective-clearing ends the objective or blocker. Allow only the initial attempt plus at most two justified retries; once exhausted, stop all further execution attempts and report unresolved. A new tool, strategy, hypothesis, agent, or delegation does not reset the budget. A new budget requires the prior sequence to stop and report, separate authorization from an accountable requester or owner, recorded evidence of a material underlying-blocker or relevant scope or system-state change, and causal rationale. Delegated work stays associated with the same objective ledger, uses its failures as evidence, and records meaningful value, blocker, a retry count matching the recorded retry depth, unresolved state, and preserved boundaries. Carry failure, reconciliation, progress, and delegation handoff evidence, and route non-blocking out-of-scope findings through an authorized channel.
11. Completion-template and completion-result schema consumers must migrate new and current records to template package 0.3.0 and completion-result schema v2.0.0, populate execution-discipline fields, and update producer, consumer, and validator selection. Repository validation treats omitted `schemaVersion` as the current major, so explicitly declare `1.0.0` on retained v1 completion-result records discovered by that validator and validate them against `schemas/v1/completion-result.schema.json`; pinned v1 consumers may remain on that path. Current/new records use the rolling or v2 schema and one `retryLedger` map keyed by objective. Store each objective's `failedOrIndeterminateOutcomes` array inside that objective's ledger and `delegationHandoff` inside every prior and current sequence, and make the outcome array non-empty exactly when that ledger contains a Failed or Indeterminate attempt; remove the obsolete top-level outcome map so the same objective cannot be split across maps. Require each retry action to record `materialChange` and `causalRationale`. Organize each objective ledger as repeatable per-objective sequences by storing only sequences that actually ended `reported-unresolved` under `priorUnresolvedSequences` and exactly one latest sequence under `currentSequence`. A terminal unresolved sequence stores earlier successful evidence in `preTerminalNonConsumingActions`, every retained action ends no later than the terminal attempt starts, and the sequence has no `nonConsumingActions`; other sequences leave the pre-terminal array empty, and further action requires a separately authorized reset sequence. Require reset evidence, including `priorSequenceId` equal to the immediately prior unresolved sequence, `authorizedAt` after its terminal end and before the current sequence starts, and causal rationale, on the current sequence when any prior sequence exists; record other successful non-consuming discovery, reconciliation, and validation actions as ledger rows; and allow an evidence-only sequence to omit `attempts` only when `nonConsumingActions` is non-empty. Require at least one `passed` validation before using validated status, require every executed passed or failed validation to record `objectiveId` equal to the exact retry-ledger key it evaluates, require at least one `Successful` ledger action for any reported `passed` validation in that same objective, and require failure action evidence in that same objective for reported failed validation. JSON Schema validates structure; full v2 conformance also requires the completion semantic checks invoked by `tools/validate-schemas/validate_schemas.py`. Record successful objective-clearing actions at their current retry position, allow `retry-authorized` only when the corresponding next retry is present, and prohibit a new sequence after objective completion. Set each per-sequence `delegationHandoff.delegated` explicitly; when true, use the containing objective ledger's outcomes as authoritative failure evidence and require `meaningfulValue`, `blocker`, `unresolvedState`, and `boundariesPreserved: true`; require `retryCount` to equal that same sequence's actual retry depth and preserve a prior sequence's handoff after an authorized reset, while the other contracts remain v1. Obtain breaking-change review before claiming migration complete; do not rewrite historical v1 records in place beyond explicit version identification needed for deterministic routing.

## Optional improvements

- C# adopters may use the direct `csharp` skill and its registered agent interface while preserving root governance and scoped `AGENTS.md` authority.
- Windows PowerShell 5.1 adopters may adopt the new legacy compatibility overlay where their project contract genuinely requires `powershell.exe`.
- Mixed-profile projects using `generate-manifest --include-profile-required` may regenerate manifests so selected secondary profiles contribute their discipline links.

## Removed or deprecated contracts

No contract is removed or deprecated relative to published `v0.10.0`.

No `v0.10.0` stable schema major path, original stable template path, stable tool entry path, or documented release artifact name is intentionally removed by this candidate. The breaking changes instead strengthen existing baseline package and profile-adoption contracts for affected adopters.

## Compatibility assumptions

- `v0.10.0` is the published migration checkpoint and source boundary.
- The candidate is breaking for existing SRE and Testing adopters and for existing canonical-profile adopters whose scope satisfies a Product Management or User Experience predicate; unaffected adopters may retain justified omissions where the updated standards permit them.
- The RC compatibility inventory at `releases/compatibility/1.0.0-rc.1.json` is the machine-readable statement of the intended stable surface.
- GOV-WORK-011 through GOV-WORK-014 are part of the active RC governance contract: the retry budget follows the objective or blocker across the whole active task or change and every tool, task, session, agent, and strategy; exhaustion is terminal; reset requires a stopped and reported prior sequence, separate accountable authorization, and recorded material blocker or relevant scope or system-state change; GOV-WORK-013 progress handling remains subordinate to the remaining budget; and delegation carries the evidence and cannot bypass the boundaries.
- Completion-result v1 to v2 and completion-template 0.2.0 to 0.3.0 are breaking changes for current consumers: rolling/current records use v2 with required `executionDiscipline`, historical or pinned v1 records remain supported unchanged, and other schemas remain v1. Producers, consumers, and validators must select the matching major rather than silently substitute v2 for historical v1.
- Repository paths can remain compatibility-sensitive even when the package they belong to is still `baseline`; baseline maturity means the package is outside the stable package promise, not that maintainers may silently break documented contracts.
- Final `1.0.0` has not yet been approved or published.

## Validation commands

```bash
python -m pip install --require-hashes -r tools/validate-schemas/requirements.lock
python -m unittest discover -s tools/tests -p "test_rc_compatibility_gate.py" -v
python tools/validate-all/run_all.py --include-tests
python tools/release/validate_release.py --tag v1.0.0-rc.1
```

The tag validation command validates the candidate contract; it does not create or publish a tag.

## Rollback or downgrade considerations

An adopter can return to the published `v0.10.0` source boundary by restoring that pin, restoring or regenerating the prior tailored composition, and revalidating derived artifacts. Do not mix RC-only requirements or maturity claims with evidence attributed to `v0.10.0`, and do not present downgrade as proof that newer readiness or testing obligations were satisfied.

## Unresolved limitations

- A source candidate does not satisfy the final `1.0.0` requirement that at least one RC be published and available for review.
- Qualified independent specialist review of the breaking package and profile changes on the exact final release head is required before merge, tagging, or publication; automated review and earlier approvals do not satisfy that gate.
- Qualified independent specialist review of the GOV-WORK-011 through GOV-WORK-014 migration is required before merge, tagging, or publication; reviewers must verify the retry ledger, terminal exhaustion, reset basis, progress or blocker narrowing, authorized out-of-scope routing, and delegation handoff evidence, and automated validation alone does not satisfy that review.
- Qualified independent specialist review of the breaking completion-template and completion-result v2 migration is required before merge, tagging, or publication; reviewers must verify current v2 execution-discipline fields, preserved v1 historical handling, producer/consumer/validator selection, and migration evidence, and automated validation alone does not satisfy that review.
- Independent artifact/checksum verification remains required before final `1.0.0`.
- Final `1.0.0` compatibility approval remains a separate independent-review gate.
- GitHub-host controls recorded as Blocked/manual remain outside repository-source validation.
